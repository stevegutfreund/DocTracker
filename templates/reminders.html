How to import

  1. In Google Calendar, open Settings → Import & export.
  2. Choose the downloaded document-expiry.ics and the calendar to add it to.
  3. After adding or renewing documents, download and import again. Events keep the same IDs, so re-importing updates them instead of making copies.

The events include names, document types and expiry dates. They never include numbers or Drive links.
